# Danger and wealth across Leonida

Note: GTA VI has no official danger or wealth rating for its areas, and none of our sources publishes one. This page reads danger and wealth from evidence: who controls an area, what lives there, and what turns up there. Treat it as a guide, not a game stat.

## No turf wars, as far as anyone knows

Rockstar has not confirmed gang wars or territory conquest, and whether players can capture or defend territory is unknown [1].

## Factions and where they operate

| Faction | Type | Territory |
|---|---|---|
| Final Chapter MC | Outlaw motorcycle club | Ambrosia County [[region:ambrosia]] [1] |
| PTT Youngin$ | Street gang | [[region:southside]], Vice City, Vice-Dale County [1] |
| San4San | Haitian street gang | Vice City [1] |
| M7 | Not stated | The junkyard in [[region:hamlet]], where they guard a rare muscle car [3] |
| Kingfishers | Not stated | The [[region:grassrivers]] swamp, per GAMINGbible, and an armed fishing shack on an island, per OutputLag [11] [4] |

## Places that can kill you

- Grassrivers: an enormous swamp, modelled on the Everglades, controlled by the Kingfishers and filled with dangerous creatures [2] [11].
- Pelican Valley: alligators in the swamp water can kill you fast [[region:pelican-valley]] [4].
- Mount Kalaga National Park: forests, lakes and hills, and a prime spot for hunting legendary creatures [2] [11].
- Hamlet: the M7 gang's junkyard [[find:gauntlet-hellfire-hamlet]] [3].
- Hyde Island: a tranquilliser dart knocks you out, you wake in a shipping container, and a hunter with night-vision goggles tracks you [[find:hyde-island-hunt]] [6].
- Port Gellhorn: in the north of town the Circles of Hell house is open <mark data-draw>from midnight to 4 AM</mark> [[find:hellspawn-satan-boss]] [7], and a missing-person poster in the Starlet Motel reception starts a serial-killer trail that leads to an abandoned trailer park in the north [[find:port-gellhorn-missing-persons]] [8].
- Mile Zero Air Station: tanks, jets and heavy helicopters, and harder to get into than GTA V's base [[region:mile-zero-air-station]] [11].

## Where the money is

Note: Wealth here is read from where rare cars turn up. It is an inference, not a rating.

- Vespucci Island: a garage hides a car that copies Brian O'Conner's green 1995 Mitsubishi Eclipse from The Fast and the Furious [[find:paul-walker-eclipse]] [5].
- Pegassi Residences in Vice City: the underground garage holds the '95 Grotti Cheetah, an Ultimate Edition car you can collect from Chapter 5 [[find:grotti-cheetah-95]] [3].

## The police are everywhere, but not everywhere the same

GTABase lists these agencies: Vice City Police Department, Vice Beach Police Department, Vice-Dale Police Department, Port Gellhorn Police Department, Key Lento Police Department, Ambrosia County Sheriff, Leonard County Sheriff and Leonard County Sheriff's Office (listed separately), Mariana County Sheriff's Office, Leonida Highway Patrol, a separate Highway Patrol Agency, and POACH (Leonida Protection of Animals and Controlled Hunting) [1].

Police only respond once a crime is reported, and after an escape they keep watching a heat zone around your last known position [9].

At the worst Criminal Profile tiers you can no longer surrender, and police are reportedly more likely to shoot on sight [10].
