# NPCs, random encounters and the living world

Note: Rockstar has published no manual-style description of NPC systems. This page describes what players and guides have documented, find by find.

## Random encounters

Some events play out whether or not you get involved, though you can interrupt them. Others are interactive: you stop and join in. GTA6Info keeps a running catalogue [1].

- Hand pills to a shady lawyer in a dumpster and he pays you back with legal advice [[find:dumpster-lawyer]] [1].
- A driver sets his own legs on fire to build a lawsuit against Sahara, and you can be paid through BuckMe for witnessing it [[find:lawsuit-burning-driver]] [1].
- A polar bear escapes a truck bound for the zoo. If you get the event after meeting a hunter, he will buy the dead bear, so <mark data-draw>the outcome changes with what you have done before</mark> [[find:escaped-polar-bear]] [1] [2].
- Operation Furball, not yet confirmed by GTA6Info: give Chase, a man in a fox suit, a ride, lose the police and get him to the Furverts' compound for $15,000 or a custom SMG [[find:operation-furball]] [1].
- Not yet confirmed: a man in a dark suit gets into Jason's car and makes him drive to Harborside Park, a nod to Tom Cruise's hitman in Collateral [[find:collateral-hitman]] [1].
- Not yet confirmed: Jimmy the Drunk Sailor tries to mug you with a speargun. Play along and he puts the Arras Bullhead speargun down where you can grab it [[find:jimmy-the-drunk-sailor]] [1].
- Steal a car with a cat inside and the cat attacks the driver in a short cinematic [[find:cat-in-stolen-car]] [3].
- Two men at target practice invite you to join and shoot objects that one of them holds above his head [[find:william-tell-target-practice]] [3].

Loud events get much longer cooldowns before they can happen again, and some never spawn again [1].

## A world that moves without you

Players report walking into car crashes, other people's heists and a plane crashing overhead, events you can ignore or join [1] [2].

## Animals

Behind Three Sheets Liquor in Key Lento, a red macaw gives away where money is hidden near a generator, during the optional Strange Noises activity. GTA6Info marks the walkthrough unconfirmed [[find:three-sheets-talking-parrots]] [4].

You can pet the cat, which has several petting animations [2].

There are 62 bird types to catalogue, a number Kotaku links to an Insider Gaming report confirmed with Rockstar [2].

## The encounter nobody can explain

Reddit user seepa-man filmed an NPC that shrugs off sniper fire from the radio tower near Jason's house in Key Lento, runs a finger across its neck, then teleports behind the player and kills them instantly [[find:immortal-sniper-npc]] [5].

It is only a chance that one NPC turns out unkillable, and GameRant says other players have shown NPCs acting the same way [5].

Note: We found no statement from Rockstar on whether it is a scripted scare or a bug.
