# Jason and Lucia, the two protagonists

Note: This page covers only what Rockstar showed before launch and the first hours of play. Story details stay out.

## Jason Duval

Jason is voiced by Dylan Rourke. Rockstar's credits, published at launch, were the first official confirmation [6].

His background, from Rockstar's official bio as summarised by GTA6Info: he grew up around grifters and crooks, joined the Army to put a troubled adolescence behind him, and ended up in the Leonida Keys working for local drug runners [1] [3]. "Jason wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder." [1]

He starts the open game living in Key Lento [1]. His first playable story mission is "Gone Fishing", and his best friend is Cal Hampton [1].

## Lucia Caminos

Lucia is voiced by Manni L. Perez, confirmed by the same launch credits [6]. GTA6Info adds that Perez also provides her likeness [2].

Her official bio, as GTA6Info gives it: her father taught her to fight, her family's roots are in Liberty City, and she is "ready to change the odds in her favor". "A life with Jason could be her way out." [2]

Her story opens with her in Leonida Penitentiary [2].

The opening is not the same for everyone: a Kotaku writer and her husband got different intro sequences depending on where and how Lucia was caught by the police [8].

## Playing as both

In free roam you play as Jason or Lucia and can split up or do activities together [3]. How freely you can switch between them outside story beats is not confirmed [1].

Each can carry two primary firearms and two sidearms at once, and weapons travel in your vehicle [3]. Owned weapons you are not carrying can be recovered from your personal vehicle's trunk or an Ammu-Nation weapon locker [7].

Each protagonist has <mark data-draw>a separate Criminal Profile</mark>, so how you play one does not change the other's [4].

Banked money is shared between them. GTA6Info states this without an outside source [5].
