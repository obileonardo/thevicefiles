# The relationship system

Jason and Lucia's relationship moves through four tiers, shown as 1/4 to 4/4 with no points score: Running on Empty, Partners In Crime, Crazy In Love and Hot Together [1].

## What raises it

- Spending free-roam time together, which GTA6Info calls the fastest way [1].
- Dates: the zoo, kayaking, beach walks and the gym [1].
- Committing crimes together, such as robbing stores [1].
- Replying to texts and calls [1].
- Playing as both characters [1].
- Small gestures: holding hands, hugging, buying food or drinks and sending selfies [1].

## What lowers it

- Ignoring texts and calls [1].
- Long stretches apart, or playing only one character [1].
- Standing your partner up or refusing them, failing a joint mission, or hitting your partner with a car [1].

## What it does not do

There are <mark data-draw>no breakups and no cheating</mark>: the lowest tier is Running on Empty [1].

Rockstar North's Rob Nelson said being lovers gives no "particular gameplay benefit", as GameSpot reported from an IGN interview and GTA6Info cites [1].

## Endings

GTA VI has multiple endings based on your actions and choices. After the credits the game returns to just before the final point of no return, so you can replay the finale for another ending [2].

Guides report that relationship tier is one input into which ending you get, and GTA6Info marks this unconfirmed [1]. The same wiki lists the Criminal Profile as another input, also unconfirmed [3].

Note: The specific mission and tier requirements reported by guides are story spoilers, so they are left out of this page.
