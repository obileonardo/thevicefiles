# Wanted level and heat

Note: Numbers and states are as documented a few days after launch. Patches may change them.

GTA VI uses six wanted stars, one more than GTA V, and the stars now carry states that tell you what the police know about you [1].

## The star states

| State | How it looks | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Cops Responding | Flashing stars | A crime was reported and police are on the way. Leave fast and they may never find you [1] |
| Suspect Unknown | Hollow stars | Police have a report but do not know who you are [1] |
| In Pursuit | White stars | Police have visual contact and know who you are [1] |
| Visual Lost | Flashing stars | Police lost sight of you and are searching. Leave the search radius unseen to clear it [1] |
| Heat | Red stars | You escaped, but police keep watching the area around your last known position, which is marked on the map. Being spotted there brings the wanted level back [1] |
| Heat and Suspect Unknown | Variant of Heat | Heat remains, but police have no description of you, your partner or your vehicle [1] |

## What the police know

Red heat icons under the stars show what police can identify: your appearance, vehicle, outfit, a visible weapon, your partner, CCTV and cloud CCTV [1].

Local CCTV footage is stored on site and can be destroyed. <mark data-draw>Cloud CCTV cannot</mark>, and an alarm eventually goes off [1].

Bloodstains and exploded dye packs can also raise suspicion. GTABase suggests a shower at a safehouse [1].

## Losing the police

- Break line of sight and hide: ladders, unlocked doors, woods and bushes all work, and you can duck in a stationary car [1].
- Shoot tyres to slow a pursuit. Helicopters may retreat if you shoot at them [1].
- K9 units can track you [1].
- Clear heat icons by changing accessories, hairstyle, outfit or vehicle, putting weapons away and splitting from your partner. Long guns cannot be concealed, so drop them or stow them in a trunk. You can keep spare outfits in your personal vehicle's trunk [1].
- Police are not shown on the minimap. Use Focus to spot them; the player arrow changes colour to show whether you are hidden [1].

## Wasted or busted

You lose pocket cash, a small BuckMe fee, unowned weapons and most likely any stash bags [1].

Owned weapons can be recovered from your personal vehicle's trunk or an Ammu-Nation weapon locker. Depositing cash at an ATM protects it [1].

## How your Criminal Profile changes it

GTA6Info says you can no longer surrender at the Psycho and Irredeemable tiers, although another section of the same page puts that rule at Irredeemable only. It also cites PowerPyx saying police are more likely to shoot on sight, without naming the tier [2].

The Criminal Profile tracks how you commit crime, from Professional through Hardened, Violent and Psycho to Irredeemable, which is permanent. Each protagonist has a separate profile [2].

## What changed from GTA V

- A crime must be reported before police respond. In GTA V, police reacted anywhere [1].
- Six stars instead of five [1].
- New: the unknown-suspect state, heat after an escape, disguises, attention from visible weapons, fuller CCTV identification and K9 units [1].
- Gone: police blips on the minimap [1].
