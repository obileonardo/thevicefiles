# Clip fill for published finds, 25 Nov 2026

52 of the 79 published finds had `clip: null`. This pass added a clip to 35 of them and left 17 without one. Every match below rests on text fetched in this session (YouTube search pages, video pages, oEmbed, the fxtwitter JSON API and the find's own source articles). No video was watched and no thumbnail or frame was looked at (Rockstar ToS 2.3, decision OS7).

## Method

- Listed the 52 finds with `jq -r '.[]|select(.status=="published" and .clip==null)|.id' content/finds.json`.
- Read each find's existing source articles for embedded or linked X, Reddit, YouTube and TikTok posts (the press often embeds the original finder's post).
- Searched YouTube (results page as text) for each find, then read each candidate's video page: channel, title, description, chapters, publish time, `playableInEmbed`.
- Checked every chosen YouTube URL with `https://www.youtube.com/oembed?format=json&url=<url>` and every chosen X post with `https://api.fxtwitter.com/status/<id>`.
- Rules applied to candidates:
  - The creator's own title, description, chapters or post text must name the same thing as the find. Where the place is named it must agree; where the find is unique (one kraken, one drunk sailor) a matching name was accepted without a place, and that is noted.
  - Rejected: credited re-uploads of someone else's footage, channels that state AI narration or "AI concept" footage, shorts built from official trailer footage or stills, compilations where the find is one item of many, cutscene uploads, and anything not embeddable.
  - Publish times were checked against the launch: the first region (New Zealand) unlocked at 11:00 UTC on 18 Nov 2026 (03:00 PST). The earliest chosen clip is 06:30 PST on 18 Nov, so none is pre-release.
- YouTube Shorts are stored with their `/shorts/` URL (the site uses it to pick the vertical player); everything else uses `watch?v=`.
- On each find only `clip`, one new `sources` entry and `updated_at` (2026-11-25) changed. 25 new creators were registered in `content/sources.json` (ids `yt-*` and `x-*`, status `active`, each with a note naming the find).

Note: titles quoted below have their dashes replaced with hyphens, because the project bans em dashes.
