# X bookmarks, round 2 (2026-11-25)

Second read of the owner's "GTA VI BUILD" bookmark folder on X. Round 1 (2026-11-23) processed 25 posts; its candidates are in `research/x-bookmarks-candidates.json`. This round covers every post added since then.

## Method

- The folder was read in Chrome, read-only (nothing liked, reposted, replied to, followed or bookmarked). It was scrolled to the end twice; both passes found the same items.
- Each new post was read as text: post text and media type from the X page and from the public fxtwitter API (`api.fxtwitter.com/<user>/status/<id>`), any quoted post, and the first replies (up to about 30) on the status page.
- Rockstar imagery rule (ToS 2.3): no screenshot, video frame or game image was looked at or analysed. Whether a post has a video or photos was decided from the page DOM and the API's media list only. Facts come from post text, replies and articles.
- Every fact in a new find was checked against a page fetched this session. A single post is the lowest level of evidence.
- No money glitches, exploits, leaks, unofficial ports, paid products or mod links were used.
